Accommodation in Kimberley


Eden Lodge provides an exceptional retreat for travelers, families, and business professionals alike. Our accommodations are designed with your utmost comfort in mind, featuring modern amenities that ensure a truly relaxing, stress-free experience. Whether you're planning a short-term getaway or an extended stay, Eden Lodge offers the perfect home-away-from-home setting.
 

Accommodations

Superior Rooms


Ideal for up to 2 adults, our Superior Rooms are thoughtfully appointed to provide a peaceful escape. Each room includes:
 

  • Choice of a luxurious king-size bed,
    or two 3/4 beds (available on request)
  • Private bathroom
  • Modern amenities such as:
     
    • Uncapped Wi-Fi
    • TV with DSTV
    • Air conditioning and ceiling fan
    • Comfortable sitting area and desk
    • Bar fridge, coffee and tea facilities
    • Daily cleaning service


Please note: Children aged 0-18 are charged 100% of the sharing rate.

Starting from R750 per room
 

Family Rooms


Perfect for larger groups or families (up to 4 adults), our Family Rooms offer versatile living spaces that cater to your needs. Each family room features:
 

  • A dedicated sleeping area with a king-size bed in one room
  • A second area furnished with two 3/4 beds and a corner lounge unit
  • En-suite bathroom
  • A comprehensive range of amenities including:
     
    • Uncapped Wi-Fi
    • TV with DSTV
    • Air conditioning and ceiling fan
    • Non-smoking environment with a comfortable sitting area and desk
    • Bar fridge, coffee and tea facilities
    • Daily cleaning service


Please note: Children aged 0-18 are charged 100% of the sharing rate.

Starting from R750 per room


General Facilities
 

Guests at Eden Lodge enjoy exclusive access to a host of on-site facilities designed to enhance your stay:
 

  • Secure and free parking
  • Dedicated braai (barbecue) facilities and a designated BBQ/picnic area
  • A welcoming bar/lounge area
  • On-site laundry services
  • A refreshing outdoor pool
  • A restaurant offering a variety of dining options
  • Comprehensive Wi-Fi coverage throughout the property


At Eden Lodge, every detail is crafted to provide a refined and welcoming atmosphere, ensuring a memorable stay whether you're traveling alone, with family, or on a business trip.
