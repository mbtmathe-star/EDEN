2. What are Cookies?


Cookies are small text files that may be placed on your device when you visit a website. 

Your connection to a website operates in a stateless manner. Among the thousands of visitors accessing a website, the platform lacks the ability to distinguish one user from another unless it utilizes cookies. Cookies serve the critical function of ensuring accurate communication between the website and its visitors. Without cookies, a website cannot reliably discern individual users amidst the crowd.
 

3. What are Cookies Used For?


Cookies are commonplace and pose no harm to your device; they merely store and/or collect website information. They enhance your online experience by remembering certain details and preferences, eliminating the need for you to re-enter information when you visit a website.
 

4. Types of Cookies
 

  • Persistent Cookies:
    Stored in your device's browser and utilized whenever you visit a website.
     
  • Session Cookies:
    Temporary cookies that remain in your browser's archive until you leave a website.


5. Additionally, cookies may be categorized as:
 

  • 1st Party Cookies:
    Set directly by the website or domain you visit.
     
  • 3rd Party Cookies:
    Set by a website other than the one you are currently on.


6. Cookies We Use on Our Website
 

  • Essential cookies to facilitate the functionality of our website and enable you to utilize all its features.
  • Temporary storage cookies to enhance your experience by temporarily storing input information in our tools and forms.
  • Analytics cookies to track website visitors and their navigation patterns, aiding in overall website functionality improvement.
  • 3rd party cookies may be utilized for remarketing and conversion tracking purposes. You will have the option to enable or disable these cookies where applicable.


7. Links to Third-Party Websites


Our website may include links to third-party websites and / or applications, including those of advertisers and / or our partners. Please note that these third-party websites may also use cookies. We do not control these websites and are not accountable for the cookies they set or access. Each third-party website will have its own cookies policy; thus, we advise you to review their policies before usage.
