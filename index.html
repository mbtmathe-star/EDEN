Welcome to Eden

Our lodge offers a relaxing stay with stylish accommodation for those seeking a peaceful retreat. Whether you are travelling for business or leisure, Eden is the perfect place to unwind, connect and enjoy.

Accommodation

The Lodge · from R750 per room

Superior rooms for two and family rooms for up to four, each with a king-size bed, DSTV, air conditioning, uncapped Wi-Fi and daily housekeeping. A peaceful home-away-from-home for travellers, families and business guests alike.
