Welcome to Eden,a delightful dwelling.Lodge, Restaurant & Conference Centre, Kimberley

Welcome to Eden Lodge and Restaurant in Kimberley, where comfort and luxury meet exceptional service. Our lodge offers a relaxing stay for those seeking a peaceful retreat, our restaurant serves family-friendly dining, and our conference centre is ready for events and meetings of any kind. Eden promises a memorable experience for all who visit.

Kimberley’s peaceful retreat

Just 5km from Kimberley Airport and 3km from the Diamond Pavilion Mall, Eden is easy to reach and easy to settle into. A lodge, a restaurant and sports bar, a conference centre and a trampoline park, all on one green and welcoming property, day and night.

Take refuge in our greenery

A sparkling pool, a braai area and quiet gardens to come back to at the end of the day, far from the crowds and close to everything.
