Accommodation: Booking, Payment & House Rules

Thank you for choosing Eden Est. To ensure a comfortable and pleasant stay for all our guests, please familiarise yourself with the following booking conditions and house rules.

1. Booking Confirmation & Payment

  • A 50% deposit of the total accommodation cost is required to secure your reservation.
  • The remaining 50% balance must be paid in full before check-in.
  • Bookings are only confirmed once proof of the initial payment has been received.
  • All outstanding balances must be settled before guests are granted access to their rooms.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations made 30 days or more before the scheduled check-in date will qualify for a refund of 25% of the total booking amount.
  • Cancellations made less than 30 days before arrival are non-refundable.
  • All cancellations must be communicated to Eden Est in writing.
  • Applicable refunds will be processed within a reasonable administrative period.

3. Check-in & Check-out

  • Check-in: From 14:00.
  • Check-out: By 10:00 on the day of departure.
  • Guests arriving after 16:00 will follow Eden Est's contactless check-in procedure.
  • Contactless check-in instructions and access details will be communicated to guests before arrival.
  • Guests are kindly requested to adhere to the stipulated check-in and check-out times.

4. Noise & Guest Conduct

  • Quiet hours are strictly observed from 22:00 onwards.
  • Excessive noise, loud music and disruptive behaviour are not permitted after 22:00.
  • Guests are expected to respect other visitors and maintain a peaceful environment throughout their stay.

5. Room Care & Damages

  • Guests are responsible for maintaining the condition of their allocated rooms and all furnishings, fittings and equipment during their stay.
  • Any damages, breakages or loss of room property caused by guests during their stay will be charged to the responsible guest.
  • The cost of repairs or replacement will be determined by Eden Est and communicated to the guest.
  • Guests are encouraged to report any existing damages or maintenance concerns upon arrival.

6. General House Rules

  • Guests must respect the property, facilities, staff and other visitors.
  • Rooms must be left in a reasonable condition upon departure.
  • Any concerns or maintenance issues should be reported to management as soon as possible.
  • Eden Est reserves the right to address any violation of these house rules.

Guest Acknowledgement

By confirming your reservation and making payment, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agreed to Eden Est's booking, payment, cancellation and house rules policy.

Thank you for choosing Eden Est. We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to hosting you!
