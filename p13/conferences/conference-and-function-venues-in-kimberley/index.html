Gather at Eden

Perfectly situated for local and international guests, just 5km from Kimberley Airport and 3km from the Diamond Pavilion Mall. Whether you are planning a corporate conference, a wedding reception or a live music event, Eden has the space for it.

Room 1

The Dynamic Hall

Live music · large events

Ideal for live music performances and large-scale events, Room 1 offers a spacious, adaptable layout that encourages interaction and enjoyment. Weddings · celebrations

Designed with productivity in mind, Room 3 is the space for business meetings, presentations and workshops. Its modern design and technology ensure a distraction-free environment, with our technical team on hand for anything you need.
